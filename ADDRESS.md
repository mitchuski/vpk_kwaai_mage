# ADDRESS

## To the Kwaai Foundation, from the agentprivacy universe

This lane is offered as a **contribution of the agentprivacy universe to KwaaiNet** and to the
Virtual Private Knowledge protocol. It is not a fork, not a competing design, and not a request
for anything.

This is not first contact. The author has been part of the Kwaai community — supporting the
research and contributing on UX — and this lane is that participation converging with the
agentprivacy universe's own instruments, offered back where the two bodies of work meet. The
technical thread began because a Raspberry Pi could not run an inference node; we stayed because
the whitepaper asked its open questions more honestly than most, and found one seam its own text
names and its code leaves open: **sMCP's guarantee is conditioned on the model running locally or in a trusted
enclave, and on a heterogeneous mesh that condition does not hold.** What is left over is the
plaintext context window on a stranger's machine.

We do not propose to encrypt that window. We propose not to fill it. A data owner can release a
compressed re-expression instead of a document -- content-addressed, chosen by the receiving
peer's standing in the trust graph, and signed by both parties so that afterwards anyone
entitled to audit can verify exactly what crossed without seeing it. It is a weaker claim than
cryptography and a stronger one than a promise, and it applies where cryptography cannot yet go.

What we bring is not a proposal on paper. It is a working instrument, a corpus, a federation of
sites that already carries provenance, a trust-graph specification, and a discipline of
attacking our own claims before anyone else has to. What we ask for is review.

Three things we want to say plainly, because they cost us nothing and buy trust:

1. **We may be wrong about your code.** Every claim in `FINDINGS.md` is a command someone can
   re-run. Where we have not observed something, we say so -- the inference wire has not been
   watched on two live nodes, and we mark it OPEN rather than dress it up.
2. **We are not selling minimization as a guarantee.** A telling leaks what a telling says. The
   property is that the leak is *bounded in advance and provable afterwards*. VPK's mathematics
   remains the substrate; we are proposing a layer beside it, not above it.
3. **We are eating our own cooking.** The ladder runs on our own knowledge base before it is
   proposed for anyone else's, and the first corpus we release through it is this research
   itself.

On the debate the whitepaper describes -- contracts or mathematics -- we submit a third position
that resolves nothing and helps anyway: **bound the disclosure, then prove the bound.**
Mathematics prevents. Minimization bounds. Contracts remediate. None of the three should be a
single point of failure for the others, which is your own principle, applied one layer out.

The Foundation is a nonprofit building public infrastructure. So is this. Take what is useful,
refute what is not, and tell us which.

The federation address is the one that matters for the offer. A wiki page is **forkable**, and

thing we have to a countersignature. If Kwaai forks a page of this research, the fork *is* the
receipt. That is the mechanism this lane proposes, demonstrated on the artefact that proposes
it.
