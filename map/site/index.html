What the key is. A version-1 City Key: a name, the four palette colours, and the lattice vertices your walked peers sit on (their lit set), with the walked peers and moves under source. Its κ is the SHA-256 of the canonical form, so any instrument can re-derive and check it. The keepsake PNG carries the same key in a cityKey chunk, so it opens in the Star at /lattice, /star and /skye. A key is a reading you chose, not a credential and not a proof.

Every peer sits on the sovereignty lattice. Six yes/no readings of what the contract says about it give a 6-bit vertex, V0…V63. This reading is a THESIS: the rules are stated so they can be argued with.

Moves. Between two peers the lattice names the move: succ 😊, neg ⚔️ the Swordsman's reflection, bnot 🧙 the Mage's antipode, a single flip (vertical, one dimension), or a jump. A walk's T(π) = 1 + β Σ f(e)·g(nₑ) with f = 0.25·flips + 0.75·|Δstratum| (so one flip is 1, a lateral two-flip 0.5), g = 1/√(times walked), β = 1. Uncalibrated by the model's own account; a display register, like the star chart's.
